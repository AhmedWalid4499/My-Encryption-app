VeilMark is installed

VeilMark is now in your Applications folder and has opened for you. You can click Close.

First steps

  1. Open Settings in VeilMark, type your name and organisation, and click Save.
  2. If macOS asks whether VeilMark may use your Documents or Desktop folder, click Allow.
  3. Drag a Word, PowerPoint or Excel file onto Embed watermark to protect it.

You can find VeilMark any time in Applications or Launchpad. Updates install themselves.
