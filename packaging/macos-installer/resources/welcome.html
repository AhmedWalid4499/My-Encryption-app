Welcome to VeilMark

This wizard installs VeilMark in your Applications folder.

What VeilMark does

  • Embed invisible, encrypted watermarks in Word, PowerPoint and Excel files and pictures.
  • Extract them again, to see who owns a document and who a leaked copy was issued to.
  • Encrypt and decrypt any file with a password.

Good to know

  • Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
  • If VeilMark is already installed, it is updated. Your name, settings and history are kept.
  • After installing, VeilMark keeps itself up to date automatically.
